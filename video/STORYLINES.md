# Akela Nahi — three storylines

Fixed for all three: 1080×1080 square for LinkedIn and X, about 50–60 s, Hindi narration by Gnani Timbre with English captions on screen, the app's real screens (captured from the running app), made-up names (Papa, Sanket). Music bed and a few action sounds.

## A. Two cities, one morning (recommended)
**Device:** a moment in someone's day. **Form:** split screen to one.

Left half: Papa's phone in his quiet flat. Right half: Sanket at his desk in another city. The film keeps both halves running at once, so you feel the distance, and closes by merging them into one frame when the son finally calls with the right question.

- **First 2 s:** split frame. Left, Papa's phone shows an ordinary call, and the caption reads “Haan beta, sab theek hai.” Right, Sanket nods and hangs up, satisfied. The word “theek” is highlighted.
- **Beats:** hook (the “sab theek hai” call) → tension (on the left only, Papa rubs his knee and skips a tablet; the right half doesn't know) → reveal (“Akela Nahi” lands across the seam) → proof (left: the app rings at 8:30, he taps Answer and talks in Hindi, with live captions; right: the update arrives — knee pain, the medicine not taken yet, “ask about his knee”) → payoff (Sanket types “Sunday aa raha hoon”; it crosses the seam, Papa hears it in Sanket's voice and replies, and the reply lands on the right) → claim (the halves merge: “It doesn't replace your call. It tells you what to ask.”) → end card (the Akela Nahi mark, “Built on Gnani AI: Prisma v3 · Timbre v3 · voice cloning”).
- **Look:** the app's own two worlds. Papa's side is the warm-dark parent app (#14110e, big serif clock); Sanket's side is the paper family page (#f4efe6). The terracotta accent (#a8461f) appears only on the seam and the highlighted words.
- **Sound:** a soft acoustic guitar and tanpura bed at about 80 bpm, near silent during the tension, opening up when the app rings. Real ringtone, a tap, a soft pop when each update lands. Gnani Timbre Hindi voice throughout.

## B. The two-minute call
**Device:** life before and after. **Form:** one frame that rebuilds.

One phone-call transcript fills the frame: “Haan beta… sab theek hai… khana kha liya… chalo rakhta hoon.” Then the same morning's Akela Nahi check-in rebuilds that frame, line by line, into what was really going on.

- **First 2 s:** a big call timer reads 1:52, with the transcript lines stacking under it. The words “sab theek hai” stay on screen.
- **Beats:** hook (the short call) → tension (the timer stops, and the lines fade to grey) → reveal (each grey line flips into a real answer from the app: “ghutne mein dard hai”, “dawai abhi nahi li”) → proof (the elder app's question screen, then the family page's facts card building in place) → payoff (the frame resolves into the “When you call, ask about: his knee” card) → claim → end card.
- **Look:** typographic. Big Fraunces type on paper, with the real app screens sliding in as the frame rebuilds.
- **Sound:** a near-silent room tone for the call, then a single piano line once the real answers appear. Key ticks as lines rebuild.

## C. The ring (the bold one)
**Device:** one object across apps. **Form:** object-to-object chain, no hard cuts.

The ringtone is the carrier. Every shot ends on the thing that opens the next: the pulsing ring becomes the green Answer button, which becomes Papa's speech bubble, which becomes a line in the summary, which becomes the WhatsApp share, which becomes Sanket's phone ringing Papa — the call that finally asks the right thing.

- **First 2 s:** extreme close-up of the pulsing terracotta ring with the Hindi label “कॉल आ रही है”, and the ringtone hits on frame 1.
- **Beats:** hook (the ring) → reveal (pull back: the parent app, “सुबह का हालचाल”) → proof (Answer → bubble → summary → message in your voice → Help alert, each one morphing into the next) → payoff (the chain loops back to a ring, now on Sanket's phone, calling Papa) → claim → end card.
- **Look:** mostly the dark parent app, with tight framing on real UI pieces at twice their real size.
- **Sound:** the ringtone's two notes become the motif of the music bed. Whooshes only on the big morphs.
